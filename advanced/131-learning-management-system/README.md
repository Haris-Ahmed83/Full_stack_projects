# Learning Management System 🎓
> Empowering education through interactive online learning experiences.

## Description
This project, a comprehensive Learning Management System (LMS), provides a robust platform for delivering and managing educational content. It enables instructors to create and organize courses, while offering students an engaging environment to consume video lessons, track their progress, and assess their understanding through quizzes. The system aims to streamline the learning journey from enrollment to certification.

## Features
*   **Course Management**: Create, edit, and organize multiple courses with structured content and modules.
*   **Interactive Video Lessons**: Seamlessly integrate video content for dynamic and engaging learning experiences.
*   **Progress Tracking**: Monitor student completion rates, lesson progress, and overall course advancement.
*   **Quiz Engine**: Develop and administer quizzes with various question types to test comprehension and reinforce learning.
*   **Certificate Generation**: Award personalized, verifiable certificates upon successful completion of a course.
*   **User Authentication & Authorization**: Secure login and registration for both students and instructors, with role-based access.
*   **Responsive Design**: A user-friendly interface accessible and functional across various devices (desktop, tablet, mobile).

## Tech Stack
*   **Frontend**: React.js, HTML5, CSS3
*   **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js
*   **Database**: MongoDB

## Key Concepts Demonstrated
*   **Courses**: Structured organization of educational content into distinct learning paths, including modules and lessons.
*   **Video Lessons**: Integration and delivery of multimedia instructional content within courses, often with progress tracking.
*   **Progress Tracking**: Mechanisms to monitor and record user advancement through course material, including lesson completion and quiz scores.
*   **Quizzes**: Assessment tools designed to evaluate user understanding and knowledge retention at various points in a course.
*   **Certificates**: Formal recognition awarded upon successful completion of a course, often dynamically generated and personalized.
